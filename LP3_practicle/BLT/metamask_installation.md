What is MetaMask?










MetaMask

MetaMask is a cryptocurrency wallet that allows users to create an Ethereum account, store and manage digital assets, and send or receive Ether.


3. Requirements
- Windows computer or laptop
- Google Chrome browser
- Internet connection
- MetaMask browser extension
- Ethereum test network (Sepolia) for practising transactions without spending real money
Important: For this practical, use test ETH on the Sepolia test network. Do not buy real ETH or transfer money just to complete the experiment.
4. Step 1: Install MetaMask








decus.medium.com


1. Open Google Chrome.
2. Visit the official website: MetaMask official website.
3. Click Download.
4. Select Chrome.
5. Chrome Web Store will open.
6. Click Add to Chrome.
7. Click Add extension.
8. Wait for installation to complete.
9. Click the Extensions icon (puzzle-shaped icon) in Chrome.
10. Pin MetaMask for easy access.
5. Step 2: Create a MetaMask wallet








binance.com


1. Click the MetaMask fox icon.
2. Select Create a new wallet.
3. Read and accept the terms if prompted.
4. Create a strong password.
5. Confirm the password.
6. MetaMask will show a Secret Recovery Phrase (usually 12 words).
7. Write it down and keep it safely offline.
8. Confirm the recovery phrase as instructed.
9. Your wallet will be created.
Security warning
Never share your Secret Recovery Phrase or private key with anyone. MetaMask support will never ask you to share it. Do not upload it here or take screenshots of it.
6. Step 3: Open MetaMask and understand the wallet








block24.ai


After creating your wallet:
1. Open MetaMask.
2. You will see your account name, such as Account 1.
3. You will see your wallet address starting with 0x.
4. Click the account address to copy it.
5. You will see your ETH balance.
6. You can use Send to transfer ETH and Receive to get ETH.
Your wallet address is public and can be shared to receive funds. Your Secret Recovery Phrase and private key must remain private.
7. Step 4: Select the Sepolia test network
For this practical, use a test network rather than the Ethereum Mainnet.








coingecko.com


1. Open MetaMask.
2. Click the network selector at the top.
3. If Sepolia is not visible, open MetaMask Settings.
4. Go to Advanced.
5. Find Show test networks and enable it.
6. Return to the wallet.
7. Open the network selector.
8. Select Sepolia.
